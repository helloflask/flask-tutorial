## Further reading

I originally wrote this book as an introduction to [*Flask Web Development in Practice: Getting Started, Advanced Topics, and Internals*](http://helloflask.com/book/1). If you want to continue learning Flask and Python web development, that book or its updated successor, [*Flask from Beginner to Advanced: Engineering Practices for Python Web Development*](https://helloflask.com/book/4) (scheduled for publication in November 2025), would be a good next step. Both books are in Chinese. Here are some other resources for further reading:

* [Official Flask documentation](https://flask.palletsprojects.com/)
* [Chinese translation of the Flask documentation](https://flask.palletsprojects.com/zh-cn/stable/)
* [The Flask Mega-Tutorial](https://blog.miguelgrinberg.com/post/the-flask-mega-tutorial-part-i-hello-world)
* [Hello, Flask! on Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/flask) (Chinese)
